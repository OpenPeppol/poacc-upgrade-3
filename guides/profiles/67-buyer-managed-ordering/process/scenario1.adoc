= Scenario 1 – Order from buyer

The following diagram shows the business process and choreography for Scenario 1.

image::images/bpmn-bmo-scenario1.png[width=1000]

[cols="1s,5"]
|====
|Scenario number
|1

|Name
|Order from buyer

|Description
a|
* An order of numbered articles or services.
* The Order states the delivery address for the seller.
* No response is returned by the seller.

|Parties involved
|Buyer +
Seller

|Assumptions
|Buyer and seller have a commercial agreement with general conditions for ordering of goods or services, including the conditions under which
an order is considered accepted without an electronic response. +
They may also have exchanged a catalogue with product information and pricing.

|The flow
a|The buyer creates an Order with one or more lines and sends it to the seller. +
The seller receives the Order and processes it for delivery, and sends no Order Response.

If agreed between the parties, the seller returns a Message Level Response ({link-bis-36}) confirming that the document was received
and identified, or reporting that it was rejected because of syntax or business rule errors.

|Result
a|
. The order is registered by the seller and is ready for delivery.
