[[prerequisites]]
= Prerequisites

Because no business response is exchanged in this process, a number of conditions that are otherwise negotiated through the response transaction
need to be settled before this BIS is used. The following prerequisites are assumed.

[cols="2s,5",options="header"]
|====
|Prerequisite
|Description

|Commercial agreement
|Buyer and seller have a commercial agreement, framework agreement or contract with general conditions for ordering of goods or services.
The agreement governs when an order becomes binding, as no acceptance is signalled electronically.

|Change and cancellation deadline
|Buyer and seller have agreed a deadline, or a rule for determining a deadline, after which an Order Change or Order Cancellation is no longer
accepted by the seller. A typical rule is “until the order is prepared for delivery”.

|Handling of the last valid version
|The seller processes the last valid version of the order, that is the initial Order as amended by the highest sequenced Order Change received
before the agreed deadline. The seller does not partially apply a change.

|Product and price information
|Product identifiers, descriptions and prices are known to both parties, for example through an exchanged catalogue ({link-bis-1} or {link-bis-64}),
a punch out session ({link-bis-18}) or the contract.

|Technical acknowledgement
|Since there is no business response, the parties should agree on the use of {link-bis-36} for technical acknowledgement of receipt and for reporting
syntactical or business rule errors. A Message Level Response is a technical acknowledgement only, it is not an acceptance of the order.

|Registration in SMP
|The receiver (seller) is registered in the Peppol network for the business process identifier of this BIS, for all three document types.
See <<profiles>>.
|====

[IMPORTANT]
====
A seller that cannot fulfil an order, or cannot apply a change or a cancellation, has no transaction available in this BIS to say so.
Such situations have to be resolved outside the Peppol process, or the parties can use {link-bis-28} or {link-bis-65} instead.
====
